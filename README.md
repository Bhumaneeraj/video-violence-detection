# Video Violence Detection: Human Behavior Analysis

Bachelor research project | Four unpublished research manuscripts

## Overview

This project explores whether cues from human behavior can help identify violence in video. The four manuscripts compare a human-behavior-analysis approach with different ways of modeling activity and motion. They are university project work, not journal or conference publications. The manuscripts describe small initial comparisons; their results should not be treated as evidence of deployment-ready performance.

## Comparisons covered

1. **Social Force Model**: examines behavioral cues alongside a model of interactions and movement within a scene.
2. **Cognitive Deep Model**: compares the human-behavior-analysis approach with a cognitive deep-learning model for video classification.
3. **Lagrangian Particle Trajectories**: looks at motion trajectories as a point of comparison for behavioral cues.
4. **Two-Stream Deep VGG**: compares human-behavior analysis with a two-stream video model using visual and motion information.

## My role

I worked on the bachelor research project and prepared the four manuscripts. The manuscripts describe data collection, analysis, and writing as the first author's responsibilities. The guides supervised the work, according to the author contribution statements.

## Status and limitations

These manuscripts are unpublished. They describe initial comparisons on small samples. The papers are shared as project manuscripts. Their reported results have not been independently validated. This repository is not a claim of peer review or a production system.
